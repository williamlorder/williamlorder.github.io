Software

Latent variable mixture models validated against Mplus, with Julia engines and R interfaces.

  • LatentMixtures.jl (Julia): latent class, latent profile, latent transition, growth mixture and two-level (multilevel) models; FIML, weights, complex survey designs, three-step methods, Mplus interoperability, NVIDIA GPU estimation.
  • latentmixtures (R): tidy interface to LatentMixtures.jl, with tables and plots.
