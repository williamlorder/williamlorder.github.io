Validation

Every feature of the package has been checked against Mplus 8 on simulated data.

  • validation/make_data.jl, validation/make_aux_data.jl and validation/make_long_data.jl simulate the datasets (deterministic; checked into validation/data/);
  • validation/mplus/*.inp are the Mplus 8 inputs and validation/mplus/*.out their outputs (Mplus STARTS = 500 100; STITERATIONS = 20);
  • the test suite (julia --project -e 'using Pkg; Pkg.test()') checks the values below without needing Mplus; validation/compare_mplus.jl and validation/compare_se.jl print side-by-side comparisons.

Known differences

  • TECH11 p-values (see the table): the LMR reference distribution here follows the published formulas; Mplus's differs and is undocumented. Prefer the bootstrap test or BIC when they disagree.
  • Convergence: LatentMixtures converges more tightly than Mplus's defaults (tol = 1e-10 per case). Parameters that are weakly identified (flat likelihood) can differ in the third decimal when Mplus stops earlier; the mg_none_tight input shows that Mplus with tight convergence reproduces the value here.
  • Random starts: Mplus uses STARTS = 20 4 and STITERATIONS = 10 by default; the defaults here are (100, 20) and 20.
  • Not implemented: covariates and sampling weights in multigroup models; categorical outcomes in growth models; class-specific Ψ or Θ in GMM; within-profile covariances on the GPU; covariates combined with LMR/BLRT; BLRT with sampling weights or a complex survey design (Mplus does not offer TECH14 with TYPE = MIXTURE COMPLEX either); the finite population correction (Mplus FINITE), replicate weights and SUBPOPULATION; and, of the two-level models: more than one random factor (K − 1 separate or correlated random class intercepts) and random slopes, random intercepts combined with between classes (KB), weights or survey designs, a categorical distal outcome (DCAT), stepwise (two-/three-step) estimation for the random-intercept model, and multilevel LTA or growth mixtures.
  • Complex designs, not compared: DCAT (Mplus refuses DCAT with TYPE = MIXTURE COMPLEX; the design-based SEs here have no reference) and TECH11 under COMPLEX (Mplus also changes its reference distribution with the design, but its mean and SD differ from ours without a design too). Deleting rows with a missing auxiliary variable recomputes n_h from the PSUs that keep a row; no validation case loses a PSU.
