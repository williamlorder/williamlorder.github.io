Codebook of the example datasets

Five small simulated datasets for the examples, tutorials and vignettes of LatentMixtures.jl and the R package latentmixtures. Load them with dataset("aces") (Julia) or data(aces) (R); both read the CSV files in this folder.

Source and licence. All data are simulated; no real person is described. The data-generating processes (DGPs) below were written for this package to mimic published findings (references at the end) and are implemented in generate.jl (seeded: Xoshiro(20261101)–Xoshiro(20261105), Julia 1.13.2). The CSV files are the canonical copies (Xoshiro streams may change between Julia versions). Data and codebook are dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0) and may be redistributed with the MIT-licensed package or on their own.

Missing values are NA in the CSV files (missing in Julia, NA in R). Raw survey or Mplus files often use a numeric code such as -999 instead; convert it before fitting, e.g. [x == -999 ? missing : x for x in A] in Julia or dat[dat == -999] <- NA in R (the Mplus validation files in validation/data/ use -999).

aces — adverse childhood experiences (n = 1,500)

Designed for: lca, multigroup LCA (group = female), one-step covariates, r3step, bch, dcat, sampling weights.

Each ACE item is missing completely at random with probability 0.02, depress with 0.04.

¹ For women the logit of ace_sex_abuse is 0.9 higher in every class (measurement non-invariance by sex; partial invariance in lca(...; group = d.female, invariance = [:ace_sex_abuse]) is the true model).

Class membership: multinomial logit with Low adversity as reference and linear predictors (a = (age − 40)/10) Household dysfunction −1.2 + 0.6·lowparentedu − 0.05a; Maltreatment −1.6 + 0.5·lowparentedu + 0.3·female − 0.10a; High adversity −2.2 + 1.0·lowparentedu + 0.2·female − 0.10a. Sample class shares 0.523, 0.199, 0.167, 0.111. The sample over-represents low_parent_edu = 1 (45% against 30% in the target population); weight = population share / sample share × exp(N(0, 0.15²)), rescaled to mean 1 (weighted class shares 0.548, 0.188, 0.163, 0.101).

Distal outcomes depend on the class only (plus sex for depress), never directly on the items, as BCH/DCAT assume: depress = class mean (4, 7, 9, 13) + 1·female + N(0, 3.5²), rounded and clamped to 0–24; srh probabilities by class (excellent … poor) Low (.25 .38 .27 .08 .02), Household (.18 .33 .32 .13 .04), Maltreatment (.14 .30 .33 .17 .06), High (.08 .22 .35 .24 .11); P(smoker) = .15, .28, .25, .40.

wellbeing_panel — wellbeing before and during the pandemic (n = 700)

Designed for: continuous lta, partial measurement invariance across waves.

id; sleep_t, activity_t, stress_t, lonely_t for waves t = 1 (2019), 2 (2020), 3 (2021), defined as in wellbeing; female, age.

DGP. Three profiles with means Thriving (sleep 7.5, activity 5.5, stress 4.0, lonely 3.8), Typical (7.0, 3.2, 6.5, 4.8), Struggling (6.0, 1.5, 10.0, 6.8) and SDs 0.8, 1.6, 2.0, 1.0 (equal across profiles and waves). At wave 2 (2020) stress is 1.5 and loneliness 1.0 higher in every profile — a wave-specific shift of the measurement intercepts, so invariance = [3 => 2, 4 => 2] (items 3 and 4 free at wave 2) is the true model. Initial shares 0.35, 0.40, 0.25; transition matrices (rows: from)

2019→2020: [.80 .15 .05; .10 .75 .15; .05 .20 .75]
2020→2021: [.85 .12 .03; .15 .75 .10; .08 .25 .67]

Monotone attrition: 6% have only wave 1, a further 10% have waves 1–2 only.

schools

3,624 students in 150 schools (8–40 per school), for two-level latent class and profile analysis (lca / lpa with level2 = school). Generated by generate_schools.jl (seed Xoshiro(20261120)), separately from generate.jl.

  • School types (between classes): supportive 0.6, high-risk 0.4.
  • Student classes (within): Low risk, Alcohol experimentation, Polysubstance use; P(class | school type) = 0.70/0.22/0.08 (supportive), 0.45/0.30/0.25 (high-risk).
  • P(behaviour = 1 | class): b_alcohol .10/.70/.90, b_binge .03/.40/.75, b_smoke .03/.15/.60, b_vape .08/.45/.80, b_cannabis .02/.15/.65, b_fight .05/.12/.35.
  • Wellbeing (normal within class): w_sleep means 8.0/7.5/6.6 (SD 0.9), w_stress 5/7/10 (SD 2.5), w_belong 3.6/3.2/2.6 (SD 0.5).
  • Every variable missing completely at random with probability 0.02.
